About Me

Who I Am

My name is Dorian Kiwanuka, and I am a senior Data Science student at Georgia State University. I enjoy learning about technology, data analytics, software development, and artificial intelligence. Throughout my academic and professional experiences, I have developed skills in programming, data analysis, automation, and problem solving. I enjoy finding ways to use technology to make processes more efficient and easier for people to use.

Why I Created This Site

I created this website as part of my web development coursework and as an opportunity to practice designing web pages using HTML and CSS. The goal of this site is to provide information about me, my interests, and the skills I have developed throughout my education and internship experiences. Creating this page allowed me to apply concepts learned in class, such as linking pages, using stylesheets, working with images, and organizing content in a professional way.

In addition to completing the assignment requirements, I wanted to create a page that reflects who I am and what I am passionate about. Having an online presence is important because it allows others to learn about my background, interests, and career goals. This project helped me understand how websites can be used to communicate information effectively and create a positive first impression.

What Motivates Me

I am motivated by opportunities to learn new technologies and solve real-world problems. During my internship experiences, I worked on automation and artificial intelligence projects that demonstrated how technology can improve business processes and save time. Seeing the positive impact of these projects inspired me to continue developing my technical skills and pursue a career in technology and data science.

My long-term goal is to build solutions that help organizations make better decisions through data, automation, and technology. I enjoy challenging myself to learn new tools and continuously improve my knowledge. This motivation drives me to seek out opportunities to grow academically, professionally, and personally.
